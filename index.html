PARTE VI · I SENTIERI DI COORG

Things to Do in and Around Madikeri

Coorg is a land of cascading waters, sacred river sources, wildlife sanctuaries, and verdant peaks. Hover or click any destination to illuminate its spot on our hand-drawn cartography.

01

Abbey Falls

8 km from Resort

Roaring waterfall tucked amidst private coffee estates and spice groves. A picturesque hanging bridge offers breathtaking views of the falls cascading into the Cauvery basin.

02

Raja’s Seat & Madikeri Fort

4 km from Resort

The seasonal garden where Kodagu kings watched sunset over endless mountain layers. Nearby sits the 17th-century granite fort and the serene Omkareshwara temple.

03

Mandalpatti Windswept Viewpoint

18 km (4x4 Jeep Trail)

An exhilarating off-road jeep ascent through Pushpagiri forests leading to cloud-kissed panoramic ridge lines that feel like the edge of the world.

04

Talacauvery & Bhagamandala

38 km from Resort

The revered birthplace of the sacred Cauvery River nestled high on Brahmagiri hills, adjacent to the Triveni Sangama holy confluence.

05

Dubare Elephant Camp & Nisargadhama

28 km from Resort

Cross the Cauvery river to observe Asiatic elephants bathing and feeding, followed by bamboo walks across the suspension bridge of Nisargadhama island.

06

Guided Estate Walks & Photography Trails

Directly on Premises

Our resident plantation guides lead you through heritage robusta blocks, explaining inter-cropping with black pepper, wild cardamom, and bird-watching vantage points.
